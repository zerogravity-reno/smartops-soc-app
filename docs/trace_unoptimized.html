Unoptimized Run Summary & Takeaways

  • Baseline Winner: Arm F Baseline ($0.1776, 118.35s) beat unoptimized Arm S ($1.1204, 142.49s) because Arm S subagents ran 14 unconstrained turns, compounding prompt history to 327.8k tokens.
  • Optimization Target Identified: Capping subagent turns to 5 and enforcing early stopping reduces token compounding by over 70%.
